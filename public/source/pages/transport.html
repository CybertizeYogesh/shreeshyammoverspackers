Land Transport Service

We also provide basic ground transportation for goods all over India, covering great distances and overcoming uneven terrain. We put in a lot of effort to ensure that we will never fall behind schedule. Our services include: relocation and movement of bulk goods and containers, and road and rail transport. Additionally, we allow the client to select groupage of other cargo and other transport solutions, such as transport of the goods in parts and whether the journey is to be covered by two different modes of transport.
